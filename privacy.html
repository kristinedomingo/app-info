Privacy Policy

This is a private app used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

The app accesses data from the owner's own accounts only to carry out tasks the owner requests.

Data is stored only on the owner's own device. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared, except with service providers as needed to carry out the owner's requests.

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their account's security settings.
